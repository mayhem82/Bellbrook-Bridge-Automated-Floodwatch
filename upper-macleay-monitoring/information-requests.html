INFORMATION ACQUISITION REGISTER

Information Requests

Active evidence-acquisition program for unresolved Upper Macleay governance, monitoring and access-restoration records.

Request standard

For NSW public authorities, requests are framed under the Government Information (Public Access) Act 2009 (GIPA Act) where formal access is required. Informal access is preferred where it can efficiently produce the same records. A request is not treated as evidence of wrongdoing; it is a controlled method for resolving documented evidence gaps.

Status rule: a request is not marked LODGED until there is evidence it was actually submitted. Until then it remains DRAFT / READY TO LODGE.

Request register

GIPA-001

Monitoring assets, station ownership and operational status

Target: Kempsey Shire Council and, where records are held elsewhere, the relevant NSW monitoring authority.

Records sought: inventories, asset identifiers, ownership/operator details, operational status, telemetry/data availability, maintenance responsibility and documented relationship between monitoring assets and Upper Macleay flood-critical crossings.

Purpose: resolve whether each critical crossing is directly monitored, indirectly represented, ungauged or unresolved.

Status: DRAFT / READY TO LODGE.

GIPA-002

Post-flood bridge inspection and reopening procedure

Target: Kempsey Shire Council.

Records sought: current and historical Standard Operating Procedures, work instructions, inspection checklists, delegations, reopening criteria, debris-clearance requirements, engineering-escalation thresholds, reopening authority and public-status publication procedure.

Purpose: resolve the procedural and delegated-authority UNKNOWN findings in Companion Evidence 08.

Status: DRAFT / READY TO LODGE.

GIPA-003

Event-level inspection, clearance and reopening timestamps

Target: Kempsey Shire Council.

Records sought: for selected 2021, 2022 and 2025 Upper Macleay flood events, records showing when water cleared each relevant structure; inspection request/allocation/completion; inspection result; debris-clearance start/completion; engineering escalation; repair activity; reopening authorisation; and publication of OPEN status.

Purpose: quantify HYDROLOGICAL CLEARANCE → OFFICIAL REOPENING latency where the underlying records permit it.

Status: DRAFT / READY TO LODGE.

GIPA-004

Inspection prioritisation and isolated-community consequences

Target: Kempsey Shire Council.

Records sought: policies, procedures, operational guidance or decision records describing prioritisation when multiple bridges/causeways require post-flood inspection, including consideration of single-access communities, medical needs, emergency-service access, essential supplies and High Flood Island consequences.

Purpose: establish whether consequence-based prioritisation exists and how it operates.

Status: DRAFT / READY TO LODGE.

GIPA-005

Upper Macleay Flood Study Council decision record

Target: Kempsey Shire Council.

Records sought: Council report, agenda attachment, minutes, resolution and associated decision record by which the relevant Upper Macleay Flood Study material was received, noted, adopted or otherwise considered.

Purpose: bind the study to the formal Council decision trail and resolve the remaining provisional governance status.

Status: DRAFT / READY TO LODGE.

Register fields once lodged

Each request will preserve: request identifier; authority; exact scope; informal/formal pathway; date lodged; acknowledgement/reference number; statutory or advised due date; extensions; decision date; access outcome; records received; withheld/redacted material and stated basis; review rights where applicable; linked Document (DOC) identifiers; and the UNKNOWN findings resolved or left unresolved.

Evidence handling

Responses are evidence regardless of whether they confirm, contradict or complicate the existing investigation. Received records are to be bound into the Official Documents & Evidence Library with stable DOC identifiers. Refusals, absence-of-record responses and redactions are recorded accurately without treating them as proof of an underlying allegation.
