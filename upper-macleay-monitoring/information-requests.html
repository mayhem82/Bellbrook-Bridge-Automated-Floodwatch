8 Records for Michael Kemp MP to obtain

Please have your office request the following records from the responsible agencies through your representations on my behalf. The identifiers GIPA-001 to GIPA-005 are retained solely for continuity with earlier versions; these are requests for your office to pursue, not formal GIPA applications lodged by me.
GIPA-001 — Please obtain the monitoring asset inventory, ownership, operational status, data types, maintenance, public access and crossing relationships.

GIPA-002 — Please obtain the post-flood bridge inspection and reopening procedures, work instructions, checklists and delegations.

GIPA-003 — Please obtain event-level inspection, clearance, repair, reopening-authorisation and publication timestamps for selected 2021, 2022 and 2025 events.

GIPA-004 — Please obtain the inspection prioritisation rules and records explaining how isolated-community, medical, emergency-service and High Flood Island consequences are considered.

GIPA-005 — Please obtain the Upper Macleay Flood Study Council report, agenda material, minutes, resolution and associated decision record.

Please identify the records holder for each item, make the request through your office and follow up outstanding responses. If records cannot be obtained through your representations, please tell me which records remain unavailable, the reasons given and what further steps your office proposes. No formal application, transmission or receipt is recorded by this revision. The response period in section 11 is a request to your office, not a statutory GIPA deadline.

11 Response requested from Michael Kemp MP

I ask your office to provide a written, item-by-item response within 28 calendar days of acknowledged receipt, setting out the representations made, records and agency responses obtained, outstanding matters and next steps. This is a requested administrative response period, not a statutory deadline. Where an agency response remains outstanding, please identify the responsible body, follow-up action and expected response date. The actual receipt date and requested due date will be recorded after delivery.

Please have your office coordinate the agency requests and retain follow-through until responses are received. For each referral, please identify the receiving body and responsible role, the matter referred, referral date, acceptance of the handoff and expected response date. Referral or acknowledgement alone does not complete the requested assistance. Please obtain each agency’s determination and supporting record, action owner, next step, funding pathway where relevant, completion criterion and target date, and report these back to me. Where an agency declines action or access to records, please obtain its reasons and supporting evidence and advise what further representations you propose. A correction should identify the proposition it changes and its source.

Please maintain an item-by-item record through your office of the agency contacted; document/version sent; delivery and receipt evidence; requested response date; response and date; whether the item is acknowledged, referred, substantively answered, actioned, refused or overdue; completion evidence; verified community effect; remaining gap; and next follow-up. Please include this status in your updates to me. Initial state: no transmission, receipt, deadline or outcome entered by this revision.
