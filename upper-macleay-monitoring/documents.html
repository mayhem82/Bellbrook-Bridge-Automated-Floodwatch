CE-10 · INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE COMPANION

Official Documents & Evidence Library

Authoritative records underlying the Upper Macleay Flood Monitoring Advocacy Package.

Purpose: this library provides the documentary layer beneath the advocacy package. Each record receives a stable DOC identifier so a printed finding can be traced through a CE evidence item to the authoritative source.

Evidence chain: Printed finding/request → CE-### companion evidence → DOC-### official record → relevant passage, page or dated publication.

No document is labelled official merely because it supports the project. “Official” means issued or published by the responsible government, statutory, emergency-management or other authoritative body. Secondary reporting remains separately classified.

Library controls

  • Stable DOC identifiers are never silently reassigned.
  • Issuing authority and document/publication date are recorded.
  • The original official source is retained where available.
  • A preserved local copy may be added where lawful and technically practical.
  • Relevant pages, passages or propositions are identified rather than relying on an unexplained document dump.
  • Capture date and evidence status are recorded when preservation is implemented.
  • If a source changes, disappears or is superseded, the record is updated without erasing the earlier evidentiary history.
  • Secondary sources are not promoted to primary evidence when a primary record can be obtained.

Preserved copies

The library is designed to hold local preserved copies of authoritative documents where lawful and practical, alongside the official source link. Preserved files have not been fabricated or implied here: a document will only receive a local-copy control after the actual file has been obtained and stored.
