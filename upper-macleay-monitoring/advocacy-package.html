INDEPENDENT ADVOCACY & EVIDENCE PACKAGE

Upper Macleay Flood Monitoring Advocacy Package

Monitoring, Isolation, Access Restoration and Community Situational Awareness

Scheduled initial presentation: Michael Kemp MP (Member of Parliament) — 30 October 2026 (not yet delivered)

Ownership and use: this is an independently authored and maintained Bellbrook resident project. Michael Kemp MP is the first formal recipient, not the owner or identity of the project. Kempsey Shire Council and relevant agencies are subsequent implementation/accountability audiences. The package is designed to remain suitable for later sharing with community representatives, agencies, councillors, media and other interested parties.

What this package is for

The package brings together the working Bellbrook Floodwatch demonstration, Upper Macleay monitoring investigation, isolation evidence, structural-harm analysis, access-restoration problem, risk assessment and implementation requests into one advocacy pathway.

The printed package is primary. It is intended to remain understandable without opening this website. This companion site supplies the deeper audit trail, evidence chain, definitions, official sources and subsequent updates.

First-stage requests — Michael Kemp MP

  • Receive the evidence package as the first formal recipient and consider the Upper Macleay issue as a network of monitoring, isolation and access-restoration problems rather than a single-bridge request.
  • Support a coordinated written response from Kempsey Shire Council and relevant NSW agencies identifying existing monitoring assets, known deficiencies, responsible bodies and practical implementation pathways.
  • Seek clarification of the documented Willawarrin warning-gauge deficiency and the status, public availability and crossing coverage of Kogal / Nulla Nulla Creek monitoring.
  • Support evidence-based assessment of genuine monitoring gaps before new hardware is selected.
  • Seek the documented post-flood bridge inspection, debris-clearance, repair, reopening and public-notification process, including prioritisation of isolated communities.
  • Request that implementation responses identify the responsible organisation, next step, funding pathway where relevant and indicative timeframe.
  • Keep the matter open for follow-through rather than treating referral to another body as completion.

Second-stage implementation — Council and agencies

  • Identify what monitoring source serves each flood-critical crossing or corridor and whether it is direct, calibrated, indirect, ungauged or unresolved.
  • Where monitoring already exists, identify what it records, who controls it, whether it is operational and whether suitable data can be made publicly accessible.
  • Establish which individual Nulla Nulla Creek Road crossings Kogal can validly represent and which require separate assessment.
  • Address the documented Willawarrin warning-gauge deficiency.
  • Assess appropriate monitoring and visual controls at repeatedly affected locations including Dowlings Falls.
  • Define the operational chain from floodwater recession through inspection, debris clearance, damage assessment, repair, reopening authorisation and publication of OPEN status.
  • Explain how single-access isolation, High Flood Island status, medical/essential-service consequences and prolonged restoration time affect prioritisation.
  • Design monitoring and public-status capability into new or substantially upgraded critical access infrastructure where appropriate.

Evidence chain

Audit trail: printed finding or request → CE-### companion evidence item → DOC-### official document → relevant page/passage or dated official record.

The Official Documents & Evidence Library uses stable document identifiers, issuing authority, publication date, official source, preserved copy where appropriate, proposition supported and evidence status. Exact passage-level binding remains an active hardening task. Until each material factual claim has a source, date and relevant passage or page, the linked register must not be described as a completed claim-level verification.

Evidence & engagement rule

This advocacy does not depend on accusations. Questions seek operational facts, records, responsibility and implementation. UNKNOWN remains unknown. Contrary evidence is retained. Relevant parties can explain, correct or supplement the record. Critical findings remain proportionate to the evidence supporting them.
