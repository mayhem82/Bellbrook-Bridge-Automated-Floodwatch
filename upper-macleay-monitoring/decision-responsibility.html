COMPANION EVIDENCE (CE) 08

Decision Points & Responsibility

Evidence-led mapping of the operational chain from monitoring through official restoration of access.

Accountability rule

This page does not assign culpability. It identifies operational decision points, records responsibility only where the available evidence supports it, and preserves UNKNOWN where the responsible body, authority or procedure has not yet been established.

Verified boundary: monitoring information, flood warnings, road closure, physical inspection, debris clearance, engineering assessment, repair, reopening authorisation and publication of OPEN status are separate functions. Evidence that one function has occurred is not evidence that the next has occurred.

Operational decision chain

01 MONITORING

Observe hydrological conditions

Function: gauges, cameras and other monitoring sources establish observable conditions.

Responsibility status: PARTLY ESTABLISHED. Existing sources include Bureau of Meteorology, NSW monitoring infrastructure and Kempsey Shire Council flood-camera/public information systems. The owner/operator and valid crossing relationship must be established for each individual source; station existence alone does not prove direct crossing coverage.

02 WARNING

Issue flood warnings and public emergency information

Function: translate observed and forecast conditions into official warning information.

Responsibility status: PARTLY ESTABLISHED. NSW State Emergency Service (NSW SES) and Bureau of Meteorology material forms part of the official warning environment. The precise warning responsibility applicable to each Upper Macleay location must remain tied to the relevant official plan and warning product.

03 CLOSURE

Close or maintain closure of affected road access

Function: determine and communicate that a Council-controlled crossing or road is unavailable.

Responsibility status: ESTABLISHED IN THE AUDITED COUNCIL RECORD FOR THE PRINCIPAL COUNCIL CROSSINGS. Kempsey Shire Council publishes closure/open status for the crossings examined in the historical restoration audit. This finding does not extend that authority to roads or assets controlled by another body.

04 INSPECTION ALLOCATION

Prioritise and dispatch post-flood inspection

Function: decide which closed structures are inspected, in what sequence and by whom.

Responsibility status: COUNCIL FUNCTION ESTABLISHED; PRIORITISATION RULES UNKNOWN. Council publications state that staff inspect structures as water falls, but the documented allocation criteria, queue management and effect of single-access, medical or High Flood Island consequences have not been established in the public evidence reviewed.

05 INSPECTION

Determine whether the structure can progress toward reopening

Function: inspect the bridge/causeway and identify defects or further work.

Responsibility status: COUNCIL FUNCTION ESTABLISHED. Council's published flood information states that a bridge will not reopen until inspection has occurred. The required inspection standard, delegated roles and record form remain to be obtained.

06 DEBRIS CLEARANCE

Remove material preventing safe restoration

Function: clear debris or deposited material where required after flooding.

Responsibility status: COUNCIL FUNCTION ESTABLISHED. Council states debris is cleared by Council staff before reopening, and the April 2021 Dowlings Falls record demonstrates that deposited material can itself prohibit access.

07 ENGINEERING / DAMAGE ESCALATION

Escalate defects requiring technical assessment or repair

Function: distinguish routine post-flood restoration from structural damage requiring engineering assessment or repair.

Responsibility status: FUNCTION ESTABLISHED; DECISION AUTHORITY UNKNOWN. Historical records show crossings, particularly Dowlings Falls, remaining closed because repair was required. The documented threshold, responsible role and engineering sign-off pathway have not yet been established.

08 REPAIR

Complete required restoration works

Function: repair damage that prevents reopening.

Responsibility status: COUNCIL ROLE EVIDENCED; DETAILED GOVERNANCE UNKNOWN. Council publications document repair activity, but procurement, escalation, completion acceptance and any external funding/asset-owner roles are event-dependent and must not be inferred without records.

09 REOPENING AUTHORISATION

Authorise return to open access

Function: make the operational decision that the crossing may reopen after required inspection, clearance and repair.

Responsibility status: UNKNOWN AT ROLE/DELEGATION LEVEL. Council publications establish that Council reports and effects reopening of the examined Council crossings, but the specific delegated officer/role, approval criteria and documentary authority have not been established in the evidence reviewed.

10 PUBLICATION

Publish OPEN status

Function: communicate restored access to residents and road users.

Responsibility status: COUNCIL PUBLICATION ROLE ESTABLISHED. Historical Council updates publish staged reopening. The elapsed time between authorisation and public publication is not consistently exposed and remains a measurement gap.

Responsibility questions requiring records

  • Who owns and operates each monitoring asset used for each critical crossing?
  • Which source is formally relied upon for warning or operational decisions at each location?
  • Who has authority to close each road, bridge or causeway?
  • Who allocates post-flood inspections and what prioritisation rules are used?
  • What inspection standard or work instruction governs reopening assessment?
  • Who determines that debris clearance is complete?
  • What defect threshold triggers engineering escalation?
  • Who accepts completed repairs as sufficient for reopening?
  • Which officer or delegated role has authority to reopen each Council-controlled crossing?
  • What record captures the reopening decision and timestamp?
  • Who publishes OPEN status and what target exists for publication after authorisation?
  • How do single-access isolation, medical needs, emergency-service access and High Flood Island status affect priority?

Evidence basis

This responsibility map is derived from the companion's monitoring evidence, Snapshot 001–012 chain, Historical Closure & Access-Restoration Audit and Official Documents & Evidence Library. In particular, Council records from 2021, 2022 and 2025 establish inspection-driven and staged reopening, debris clearance and damage/repair pathways.

Control rule: where the evidence establishes that an organisation performs a function but does not establish the individual delegated decision-maker, this page records the organisation-level function and leaves the role/delegation UNKNOWN.
