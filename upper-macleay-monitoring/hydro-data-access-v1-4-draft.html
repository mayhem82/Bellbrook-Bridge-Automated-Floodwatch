04 · Public information responsibility.

The NSW flood-data program identifies local government as retaining responsibility for providing flood information to the public. Platform custody, dataset publication, legal access decisions and technical delivery are distinct responsibilities.

NSW Flood Data Access Program

NSW GIPA — relevant safeguards

Section 53 of the Government Information (Public Access) Act 2009 concerns reasonable searches for information held by an agency. Section 72 concerns the form of access following a formal access decision, subject to statutory exceptions. Neither provision establishes a universal maximum dataset size or automatically requires creation of a new simplified model or customised analysis.

A dataset's listing in a catalogue, its permission status, an agency's reasonable search and a formal decision about a requested delivery format must be assessed separately.

Evidence still required

  1. Complete authoritative resource inventory: filenames, sizes, versions, formats and download permissions.
  2. Original access classification, dates, responsible organisation, authorisation and recorded rationale.
  3. Whether ordinary users can retrieve selected files, geographic subsets or individual flood events without full archives.
  4. Existing alternative arrangements: inspection, separate files, physical media or another accessible delivery method.
  5. Which agency holds and can search the relevant original model files and permission records.

These are evidence-acquisition targets, not claims that particular records exist or have been withheld.

Proportionate institutional pathway

Kempsey Shire Council: clarify dataset ownership, classification decisions and existing alternatives. NSW SES Flood Data Access Team: clarify portal functionality, permission workflow and technical limitations. NSW IPC: provide GIPA guidance or review an eligible formal access decision. State Records NSW: clarify recordkeeping obligations. Other oversight bodies should receive a systemic referral only if the evidence later supports one.

No correspondence has been sent as part of this addendum. No finding of wrongdoing has been issued.

Remedy under consideration — not a demand for new modelling

Publish a reliable file-level inventory and access instructions; explain any restriction; and identify existing practical ways to obtain relevant material while retaining original model files, metadata, assumptions and scientific provenance. If selective retrieval is technically unavailable, disclose that limitation and the available alternative.
