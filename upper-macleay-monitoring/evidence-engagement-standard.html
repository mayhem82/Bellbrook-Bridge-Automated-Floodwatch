INDEPENDENT UPPER MACLEAY FLOOD MONITORING EVIDENCE COMPANION

Evidence & Engagement Standard

The conduct standard governing how evidence, questions, corrections and responses are handled throughout this independent work.

Core standard: lawful, respectful, evidence-led engagement without entrapment, predetermined culpability or manufactured conclusions. Accountability follows the evidence.

Operating principles

No entrapment

Questions seek information, explanation, responsibility, records or action. They are not constructed to manufacture an admission or trap a respondent into a predetermined conclusion.

No predetermined culpability

A monitoring gap, delay, missing record or unanswered question is not automatically treated as misconduct. Motive and culpability are not inferred without evidence capable of supporting that conclusion.

UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN

Missing evidence is classified as unknown or unresolved. It is not converted into proof of absence, proof of wrongdoing, a zero-risk assumption or an adverse conclusion.

Fair opportunity to explain

Michael Kemp MP (Member of Parliament), Kempsey Shire Council, agencies and other relevant parties may explain, clarify, correct or supplement the record. A supported response is incorporated even where it changes an existing finding.

Contrary evidence is preserved

Evidence that conflicts with an existing interpretation is not discarded because it is inconvenient. The conflict is recorded, assessed and resolved where the evidence permits.

Facts, inference and questions remain separate

Documented facts are distinguished from interpretation, unresolved evidence gaps and requests for clarification. A question is not presented as though its premise has already been proved.

Context is retained

Official documents and statements are represented with sufficient context to avoid changing their meaning through selective quotation or extraction.

Institutional focus

Where the issue concerns a system, procedure or organisational responsibility, unnecessary attribution to individual staff members is avoided.

Corrections are appendable and visible

Material corrections or stronger later evidence should update the active finding while preserving the investigation's continuity record. The snapshot chain is not silently rewritten.

Responses are evidence

Substantive responses from representatives, Council or agencies can become part of the evidence record. They may confirm, qualify, resolve or contradict an existing finding.

Question design

Preferred form: ask for the operational facts and records needed to reconstruct what occurred.

For example, rather than presupposing that residents were improperly left isolated after floodwater receded, the investigation asks:

What operational steps apply between hydrological clearance and reopening, and what were the recorded times for inspection, debris clearance, any required repairs, reopening authorisation and publication of the OPEN status?

This permits the evidence to establish the duration and cause of any delay without embedding an accusation in the question.

Use of this standard

This standard applies to the companion evidence site, printed submission material, meeting questions and answers, subsequent correspondence, evidence updates and later sharing of this work. It does not prevent critical findings. It requires those findings to remain proportionate to the evidence supporting them.
