Bellbrook automated floodwatch

Bridge gauge, clearance, and crossing status.

Uses a current mirror of Bureau of Meteorology river-height observations for Bellbrook station 059122, compares the latest valid reading against the 2.80 m bridge deck, and refreshes every 5 minutes. Treat this as a local decision aid and verify against official warnings and the live camera during flood conditions.

Install Floodwatch: The same install button works on supported phones and desktop computers. On Android, the Floodwatch icon may appear in your Apps screen/app drawer rather than directly on the Home screen. On a PC using a supported browser such as Chrome or Edge, choose Install Floodwatch; the installed app is available from your computer’s apps/start menu and can be pinned or have a desktop shortcut created. The installed app uses the Floodwatch bridge-and-water icon.

Reading looks stale? Open “Check official BOM Bellbrook table” and compare the latest timestamp for station 059122. This lets you see whether BOM itself has not published a newer Bellbrook observation or whether Floodwatch needs refreshing.

The gauge card is deliberately plain and high-contrast so it can be read fast on a phone in poor weather.

Bellbrook Bridge Automated Floodwatch. River-height observations: Bureau of Meteorology data via the current OpenDataSoft mirror, station 059122. Deck reference: 2.80 m. All displayed observation times are AEST (UTC+10). Status thresholds are local dashboard thresholds, not a substitute for emergency services advice.
